# Dataset Datasheet: Preliminary Institutional Extract and Planned Thesis Dataset

## Motivation
- **Purpose:** The source records were originally created for routine academic and administrative processes. A confidential preliminary extract of these real institutional records is currently available to assess data availability, refine the research scope, and design the future thesis dataset. The definitive analytical dataset has not yet been constructed.
- **Created by / funded by:** The source records were generated by the academic and administrative units of one higher education institution. Margorie Fernanda Pérez Simba will construct the definitive research dataset from authorized institutional sources for doctoral research. Funding information: UNKNOWN, to be confirmed.

## Composition
- **What instances represent:** The preliminary extract contains historical student records across academic periods and may include multiple operational records for the same student. The definitive unit of analysis will be established after the prediction point and longitudinal representation are finalized; it may be one student at a defined prediction point or an authorized temporal sequence of student records.
- **Variables:** The preliminary extract contains real structured academic, administrative, contextual, and temporal fields. The definitive dataset may incorporate additional authorized variables, newer cohorts, and textual sources required by the final research scope. The final record count and predictor list are therefore not yet fixed.
- **Labels:** The planned binary outcome is dropout or continued enrolment. Dropout will be derived from institutional registration records across the four regular academic periods following the student's first registration. A change of degree program within the same institution will not be labeled as dropout.
- **Sample vs population:** The preliminary extract contains selected historical cohorts from one higher education institution in Ecuador and is not the definitive thesis sample. Final inclusion and exclusion criteria will be applied when the analytical dataset is constructed. Neither the current nor future institutional dataset represents all higher education students in Ecuador or other institutions.
- **Missing data:** Operational records may contain missing or incomplete values. Their amount, pattern, possible meaning, and treatment have not yet been finalized and will be documented for each version of the definitive analytical dataset.

## Collection process
- **How collected:** The source data were recorded through routine institutional processes such as admission, registration, academic progression, and student support. A preliminary extract is available through the researcher's institutional work context. Construction and use of the definitive thesis dataset will require the corresponding institutional authorization and data-governance procedures.
- **Timeframe:** The preliminary extract contains historical cohorts and multiple regular academic periods. The final timeframe is not fixed because it will depend on when model development begins, which cohorts meet the eligibility criteria, and whether the four-period outcome window is complete. Newer cohorts may be added when eligible.
- **Consent / ethics:** The records were collected for institutional academic and administrative purposes rather than specifically for this study. Their secondary use for doctoral research requires formal institutional authorization, confidentiality safeguards, and ethics review when applicable. Final approval status: UNKNOWN, to be confirmed before definitive data preparation or model training.

## Preprocessing / cleaning / labeling
- **Steps:** No final preprocessing pipeline has been applied as part of this course documentation. The planned procedure will remove or pseudonymize direct identifiers, check duplicates and invalid values, document missingness, construct the dropout label from registration records, and define the prediction point before modeling. Data will be split by the planned validation design before feature selection, imputation, encoding, scaling, or class balancing to prevent data leakage.
- **Raw data available:** The raw institutional records are not publicly available because they contain confidential student information. Access is restricted to authorized personnel under institutional data-protection procedures.

## Uses
- **Appropriate uses:** At this stage, the preliminary extract may be used under institutional authorization to understand data structure, availability, and quality and to refine the research design. Once the definitive dataset and approvals are in place, appropriate uses will include student dropout prediction research, evaluation of explainability and fairness, analysis of temporal patterns, and development of preventive decision support under human supervision.
- **Should NOT be used for:** The dataset should not be used for public identification of students, punitive decisions, automatic denial of admission or financial aid, academic sanctions, commercial profiling, or research unrelated to the approved purpose. It should not be used to make claims about other institutions or national student populations without additional validation.

## Distribution
- **How distributed:** Individual-level student records are confidential and will not be uploaded to GitHub or another public repository. Access will remain limited to authorized personnel in controlled environments. Only non-identifying metadata, aggregated results, or institutionally approved examples may be shared publicly.
- **License / restrictions:** The dataset does not have an open-data license. Its use is subject to institutional authorization, confidentiality requirements, data-protection policies, and any applicable ethics approval. The specific data-use agreement and retention conditions are UNKNOWN and must be formally confirmed.

## Maintenance
- **Maintainer:** The institutional units responsible for the source information systems remain the data stewards. Any authorized research copy and its documentation will be managed by Margorie Fernanda Pérez Simba in coordination with the relevant institutional units.
- **Update plan:** The definitive dataset may incorporate additional authorized variables, newer academic periods, and eligible cohorts as the doctoral scope is finalized. Each research version will document its extraction date, included cohorts, eligibility rules, variable definitions, prediction point, outcome construction, and preprocessing changes. Data quality, subgroup representation, and temporal drift will be reviewed after each update.
