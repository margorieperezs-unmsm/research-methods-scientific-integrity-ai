# Dataset Datasheet: Institutional Student Dropout Prediction Data

## Motivation
- **Purpose:** The source records were originally created to support routine academic and administrative processes. The research dataset was assembled to develop and evaluate models for early student dropout prediction and to support transparent, preventive retention decisions.
- **Created by / funded by:** The source records were generated by the academic and administrative units of one higher education institution. The research dataset was assembled by Margorie Fernanda Pérez Simba for doctoral research. Funding information: UNKNOWN, to investigate.

## Composition
- **What instances represent:** Each analytical instance represents one student at the defined prediction point and combines information from institutional academic and administrative records.
- **Variables:** The current dataset contains structured academic, administrative, contextual, and temporal variables. The exact number of records and final predictor list must be documented after the analytical dataset is finalized.
- **Labels:** Binary outcome: dropout or continued enrolment. Dropout is determined from registration status during the four regular academic periods following the student's first registration. A change of degree program within the same institution is not labeled as dropout.
- **Sample vs population:** The dataset represents selected historical student cohorts from one higher education institution in Ecuador. It does not represent all higher education students in Ecuador or other institutional contexts.
- **Missing data:** Some variables may contain missing or incomplete values because they originate from operational information systems. The amount, pattern, and treatment of missing data must be documented before final model training.

## Collection process
- **How collected:** The source data were recorded through routine institutional processes, including admission, registration, academic progression, and student support activities. Authorized personnel extracted and integrated the relevant records from institutional information systems for research analysis.
- **Timeframe:** The dataset contains historical academic cohorts and multiple regular academic periods. The exact initial and final periods included in the definitive dataset must be documented after the cohort selection is finalized.
- **Consent / ethics:** The records were originally collected for institutional academic and administrative purposes rather than specifically for this research. Secondary research use requires institutional authorization, confidentiality safeguards, and ethics review when applicable. Final approval status: UNKNOWN, to be confirmed before the definitive study.

## Preprocessing / cleaning / labeling
- **Steps:** Student identifiers are removed or pseudonymized. Records are checked for duplicates, inconsistent formats, invalid values, and missing information. Categorical variables are encoded, and numerical transformations are fitted using training data only. The dropout label is created from institutional registration records according to the four regular academic periods following first registration. Data splitting is performed before feature selection, imputation, scaling, or class balancing to prevent data leakage.
- **Raw data available:** The raw institutional records are not publicly available because they contain confidential student information. Access is restricted to authorized personnel under institutional data-protection procedures.

## Uses
- **Appropriate uses:** Research on student dropout prediction, development and validation of institutional decision-support models, analysis of temporal dropout patterns, evaluation of explainability and fairness, and prioritization of preventive student support under human supervision.
- **Should NOT be used for:** The dataset should not be used for public identification of students, punitive decisions, automatic denial of admission or financial aid, academic sanctions, commercial profiling, or research unrelated to the approved purpose. It should not be used to make claims about other institutions or national student populations without additional validation.

## Distribution
- **How distributed:** The dataset is stored and processed within controlled institutional environments. Access is limited to authorized researchers and institutional personnel. Individual-level records will not be uploaded to GitHub or another public repository. Only aggregated results, metadata, or approved synthetic examples may be shared publicly.
- **License / restrictions:** The dataset does not have an open-data license. Its use is subject to institutional authorization, confidentiality requirements, data-protection policies, and any applicable ethics approval.
